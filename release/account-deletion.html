What deletion removes

Deletion removes your Supabase account and personal account-linked data, including your profile, private pantry, saved recipes, meal plans, shopping list, and cooking history. Data stored by external providers may be subject to their own backup and log retention periods.

Shared household data

Shared household pantry and shopping-list entries can remain visible to other members after your account is deleted. Your creator attribution is removed. If you own a household with other members, ownership is transferred to an existing member so their shared kitchen can continue.
