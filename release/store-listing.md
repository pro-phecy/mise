## Full description

Dinner starts with what you already have.

Add ingredients from your fridge or pantry and mise suggests meals that fit. Scan a fridge photo, add items by hand, or search for something you are craving. Then adjust servings, cooking time, dietary preferences, equipment, and skill level to make the recipe yours.

MAKE THE MOST OF YOUR KITCHEN
• Generate recipes from ingredients you want to use.
• Ask for a specific dish, remix a recipe, bake something sweet, or mix a drink.
• Build a weekly meal plan around your preferences and schedule.
• Turn planned meals into a shared or personal shopping list.
• Save recipes, organize collections, and follow step-by-step cooking mode.
• Track pantry quantities and expiry dates, and scan packaged-food barcodes.
• Set dietary preferences, allergies to avoid, cooking equipment, servings, and nutrition targets.
• Estimate meal calories from a photo or review nutrition details when available for a barcode product.

AI recipe and nutrition suggestions are estimates and can be incorrect. Always review recipe instructions, cook food safely, and check packaged product labels for ingredients and allergens. mise is not a medical or nutrition service.

Create an account to sync your pantry and recipes across devices. Your personal pantry stays private unless you choose to share it with a household kitchen.
